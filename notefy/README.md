# notefy

**Write drum grooves the easy way, without having to read sheet music.**

notefy is a drum-kit notation app for drummers who think in grooves, not notes. You click hits into a step sequencer and notefy turns them into proper drum notation in real time, ready to print as a PDF, drop into your DAW as MIDI, or send to a friend as a link.

▶ **Open the app:** [radimusi.github.io/notefy](https://radimusi.github.io/notefy/)

It runs in the browser on desktop and phone. There's nothing to install and no account to create.

---

## What it does

### Sequencer
- Click or drag across the grid to write a groove.
- **Select several notes** (hold a note, or Shift-click), then accent, ghost, flam or drag them all at once, or delete, copy and paste them.
- **Undo / redo** for every change.
- The kit covers kick, snare, hi-hat (closed, open and pedal), three toms, ride, ride bell, ride crash, two crashes, a stack, cowbell and clap. It also has rimshot, cross-stick and rim clicks on the snare and toms. Extra instruments appear only when you add them, and "Used only" hides every row the groove doesn't use.
- Five stroke types: **normal, accent, ghost note, flam and drag**.
- Each beat can have its own subdivision: 16ths, 32nds, triplets or sextuplets.
- Time signatures: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8 and 12/8.
- **Swing** on 8ths or 16ths, and **microtiming** per instrument, so you can push or lay back a part by a few milliseconds.

### Notation
- Standard drum-kit notation, laid out following the PAS / Weinberg drumset notation guide.
- Accents, ghost notes, open hi-hat, flams, drags, rimshots and rim clicks all show up in the score.
- **Sticking:** tap below a note to add R / L, or select a run of notes and apply a rudiment pattern such as paradiddles, double strokes or roll patterns, with a right- or left-hand lead.

### Playback
- Real sampled drums, recorded at several dynamic levels so accents and ghost notes actually sound different.
- Loop the whole groove or a single bar.
- A metronome in quarter notes or 8ths, and a slider to balance the click against the groove.
- "Follow" keeps the playing line in view while the groove plays.

### Save, share & export
- **Library:** save grooves into folders, then search, rename and reorganise them.
- **Share:** copy a link that contains the whole groove. Anyone who opens it can play it and save their own copy, and no account is needed.
- **Export PDF:** a print-ready sheet with title, tempo and a legend.
- **MIDI export and import:** export a `.mid` file with note mappings for General MIDI (Logic, GarageBand, EZdrummer, Superior Drummer, Addictive Drums), an Ableton 16-pad Drum Rack, or your own custom map. You can also load a drum MIDI file straight into the grid.
- **Import PDF:** load a groove back from a PDF that notefy exported.
- It comes with built-in example grooves: a laid-back funk, a 6/8 ballad and a 12/8 blues shuffle.

---

## Good to know

- Your library is stored **in your browser** on the device you use. Nothing is uploaded, and the library won't appear on another device or browser. To move a groove to another device, send yourself a share link.
- A share link holds the groove as it was when you shared it. If you change the groove afterwards, share a new link.
